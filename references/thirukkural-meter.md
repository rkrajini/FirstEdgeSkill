# Reference: Thirukkural Couplet & Moral Meter (திருக்குறள் வெண்பா யாப்பு)

## Overview
Authored by Thiruvalluvar, the *Thirukkural* (திருக்குறள்) is the apex of succinct philosophical couplets. When an image contains serene, moral, or thought-provoking human moments, applying the Kural meter distills immense wisdom into two tightly structured lines.

## The Kural Venba Structure (குறள் வெண்பா இலக்கணம்)

### 1. Seven Cir Meter (ஏழு சீர்கள்)
- **First Line**: Exactly 4 *Cir* (metrical words / feet) (முதல் அடி - நான்கு சீர்).
- **Second Line**: Exactly 3 *Cir* (metrical words / feet) (இரண்டாம் அடி - மூன்று சீர்).
- Total: 7 words packed with layered allegory and moral insight.

### 2. Monai & Ethugai Placement
- Line 1 and Line 2 share matching second-letter harmony (**Ethugai**).
- Internal words within the first line often feature matching first-letter alliteration (**Monai**).

### 3. Philosophical Depth
- Connect the visual scene (e.g., a candle in dark room, a flowing stream, an ancient tree, a farmer in soil) to a universal life lesson: patience, virtue (அறம்), inner beauty (அன்பு), or resilience.

## English Pairing Tone
- Aphoristic, pithy two-line epigram or reflective philosophical couplet (similar to Marcus Aurelius or Alexander Pope's heroic couplet).
- High density of meaning per word.
