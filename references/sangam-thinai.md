# Reference: Sangam Thinai Poetic Landscape System (சங்கத் திணை மரபு)

## Overview
In classical Sangam Tamil literature (சங்க இலக்கியம்), human emotion and internal poetry (*Agam* / அகம்) are intrinsically tethered to five distinct physical landscapes (*Aintinai* / ஐந்திணை). When writing a poem based on an image, map visual cues to the closest Thinai.

## The Five Thinai (ஐந்திணைகள்)

### 1. Kurinji (குறிஞ்சி) – Mountainous Regions (மலையும் மலை சார்ந்த இடமும்)
- **Visual Elements**: Mist, mountains, waterfalls, lush hills, bamboo forests, wild flowers (Kurinji flower).
- **Core Emotion / Theme**: Union of lovers, intense longing, sweet anticipation, awe of nature.
- **Color Palette**: Emerald greens, misty blues, deep earth browns.
- **Poetic Imagery**: The roar of waterfalls like thunder, solitary summits, hidden caves.

### 2. Mullai (முல்லை) – Forest & Pasture (காடும் காடு சார்ந்த இடமும்)
- **Visual Elements**: Woodlands, rainy twilight, cattle grazing, wildflowers, dusk, pastoral life.
- **Core Emotion / Theme**: Patient waiting, quiet domestic affection, loyalty during separation.
- **Color Palette**: Golden twilight, wet bark, twilight indigo, soft pasture greens.
- **Poetic Imagery**: The evening jasmine blooming after gentle rain, herds returning home.

### 3. Marutham (மருதம்) – Agricultural Plains (வயலும் வயல் சார்ந்த இடமும்)
- **Visual Elements**: Lush paddy fields, rivers, canals, lotus ponds, morning dew, village life.
- **Core Emotion / Theme**: Reconciliation, mild lover's quarrel (ஊடல்), peaceful abundance, fertility.
- **Color Palette**: Fresh paddy green, radiant sunrise gold, water reflections.
- **Poetic Imagery**: Swans skimming over lily ponds, ripe grain swaying in the morning breeze.

### 4. Neythal (நெய்தல்) – Coastal Seashores (கடலும் கடல் சார்ந்த இடமும்)
- **Visual Elements**: Ocean waves, sandy beaches, sunset on the horizon, fishermen boats, seagulls.
- **Core Emotion / Theme**: Deep yearning, grief of separation, waiting for return across vast waters.
- **Color Palette**: Cerulean blues, white seafoam, fiery sunset orange, dusk grey.
- **Poetic Imagery**: Waves erasing footsteps on the shore, lonely lamps on distant boats.

### 5. Paalai (பாலை) – Arid Desert / Wasteland (வறண்ட நிலமும் சுடுமணலும்)
- **Visual Elements**: Sun-baked roads, dry trees, cracked earth, blazing sun, desolate paths.
- **Core Emotion / Theme**: Endurance through adversity, perilous journey, separation for duty or livelihood.
- **Color Palette**: Ochre, amber, bleached bone, blazing sunlight.
- **Poetic Imagery**: Mirage dancing in shimmering heat, hardy shrubs holding life against drought.

## Poetic Composition Guidelines
- Identify the predominant Thinai in the user's photograph.
- Reflect the corresponding nature imagery in both English and Tamil stanzas.
- In Tamil, use rich Sangam nature vocabulary (e.g., விடியல், அருவி, அலை, பொழில், காடு, புனல்).
